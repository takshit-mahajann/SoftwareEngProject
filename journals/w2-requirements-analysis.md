# Week 2 — Requirements Analysis

## Work Done

- Identified the pharmacy owner/authorized staff as the primary user.
- Identified functional requirements for authentication, medicine management, inventory, suppliers, purchases, customers, billing, notifications and analytics.
- Defined requirements for the AI chatbot.
- Prepared user stories describing expected system interactions.
- Classified requirements into core, supporting and future intelligent requirements.
- Considered non-functional requirements such as usability, maintainability, security, scalability and extensibility.

## My Contribution

I contributed to documenting the requirements and understanding the interface requirements for the major modules.

The functional and non-functional requirements of the project were defined and the expected system workflow became clearer.
