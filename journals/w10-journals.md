# Weekly Project Work Journal – PharmaFlow AI

**Project Name:** PharmaFlow AI – AI-Powered Pharmacy Management System  
**Week:** 28 October 2026  
**Work Completed:** Customer Billing, Supplier Billing and Invoice Generation

## 1. Objectives
- Extend the existing pharmacy management system with billing functionality.
- Implement billing features for customers and suppliers.
- Add invoice generation to maintain proper transaction records.

## 2. Work Completed
- Added **customer billing functionality** to record medicine sales and calculate the bill amount based on medicine quantities and prices.
- Added **supplier billing functionality** to maintain records of medicine purchases and supplier-related transactions.
- Integrated **invoice generation** to provide a structured record of billing transactions.
- Worked on integrating these features with the existing PharmaFlow AI system.
- Focused on improving the organization of sales and purchase records and reducing manual billing calculations.

## 3. Learning Outcomes
- Understood the role of billing and invoice management in pharmacy operations.
- Learned how customer sales and supplier purchases can be handled through separate billing workflows.
- Gained a better understanding of integrating new modules into an existing software project.
- Recognized the importance of maintaining accurate and organized transaction records.

## 4. Challenges and Considerations
- Ensuring consistency between billing information and medicine details.
- Maintaining accurate calculations for quantities, prices and total amounts.
- Ensuring that invoice details correspond to the correct transaction records.

## 5. Next Week's Plan

- Identify and fix any errors found during testing.

This week, the PharmaFlow AI project was extended with customer billing, supplier billing and invoice generation features.
